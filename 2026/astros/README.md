*Astros* is a living, luminous meditation on the present sky. An ever-changing translation of astrological data into light and sound. Drawing from real-time celestial positions, the work renders the cosmos as it is now, transforming astronomical precision into a poetic visual language.

The sky also sings. Four voices play small, light melodies on a single shared scale, so any of them can sound with any other without clashing. The Sun is always present, telling its theme according to the house and sign it inhabits, over a slow pedal shaped by the Moon. The planets awake as a reading sweeps around the wheel and join the voice that matches the temperament of their aspects: union, harmony or tension. When they fall behind, they linger as echoes. The result is never the same twice: a quiet, ever-shifting music, and a pianola of the heavens.
