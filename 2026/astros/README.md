*Astros* is a living meditation on the present sky, an interactive web piece that translates real-time celestial positions into a luminous, ever-changing image: astronomical data rendered alongside astrological symbols, shifting as the cosmos shifts.

Two knowledge systems meet here over the same sky. Astronomy tells you *where* the planet is: its position calculated from orbital mechanics, precise, quantitative, indifferent to meaning. Astrology tells you *what it means*: a language that has accumulated interpretive weight across millennia, culturally encoded, reading the same positions as portent and symbol. *Astros* refuses to resolve the tension between them. Both are rendered simultaneously, neither canceling the other. The sky becomes a surface that two incompatible codes claim at once. What appears is not a synthesis but a coexistence: the cosmos held in form by two languages that do not agree.

Drawing from live orbital data through the [Hypatia](https://github.com/patriciogonzalezvivo/hypatia) astronomical library, the work renders the sky not as myth or memory but as a present condition, measured and translated in real time, and still irreducibly full of meaning.

Made in conversation with [Martin Bonadeo](https://martinbonadeo.art/), [Oliverio Duhalde](https://www.oliverioduhalde.com/) (sound design), and Alejandra Eusebi Polich (astrology).
