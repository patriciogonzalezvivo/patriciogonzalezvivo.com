For me, art making is a way of encoding meaning into matter. I believe artists draw from two essential sources: the visible world and their own inner life. When the binding is true, the chosen medium transcends its physical limits to carry an intrinsic, internal truth.

I bridge analog and digital languages, convinced that the true nature of my work emerges within the friction of their connection. My current practice spans oil painting and real-time digital simulation. This approach allows me to explore procedural, algorithmic code alongside traditional artistic practices whose origins trace back to the European Renaissance.

Every medium carries its own expressive resonance. Oil holds light and time differently than code does; each allows me to say something the other cannot. The medium is never a neutral vessel. It acts as the binder through which intent is structured, fundamentally shaping the very meaning it carries.
