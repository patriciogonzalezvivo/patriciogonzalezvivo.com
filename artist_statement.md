For me the creation of art is both a process of exploration and discovery, it's a gradual unfolding of an idea, like peeling back the layers of a sedimentary rock, revealing the history embedded within. The meaning is reveal from within, as we build up layers of meaning. 

In that process for me the tools are integral part, there is some knowledge I can access to if I haven't build the instruments to access it. Like a telescope, or a microscope, or a paintbrush, or a camera, or a pen. They are extensions of our senses, they allow us to see and understand the world in new ways. They all affect the way we see and understand the world. The way we construct meaning. 

Like the charcoal pens that sketch the first lines of a drawing, that later the brush completes. Some of my tools are digital, like Hypathia, my astronomical library, or Lygia, my graphical library, both respectively help me percive and simulate the cosmos. They are the fertil ground for imagining artifacts, rituals and symbols that later materialize in artworks.
